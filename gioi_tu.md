Tổng kết :
1. IN / ON / AT
IN → năm / tháng / mùa / buổi
→ in 2026
→ in October
→ in summer
→ in the morning

ON → ngày / thứ
→ on Monday
→ on December 15th
→ on Monday morning

AT → giờ / thời điểm
→ at 9:30
→ at noon
→ at night
→ at midnight

2. FOR / SINCE
FOR → khoảng thời gian
→ for five years
→ for three months

SINCE → mốc thời gian
→ since 2020
→ since Monday

3. DURING
DURING + sự kiện / khoảng thời gian
→ during the meeting
→ during the holiday
→ during the training

4. BY / UNTIL
BY → hạn chót
→ by Friday
→ by 5 p.m.
→ by the end of the month

UNTIL → kéo dài đến
→ until Friday
→ until 10 p.m.
→ until Monday
5. BETWEEN / AMONG
BETWEEN → giữa các đối tượng riêng biệt
→ between A and B
→ between the two departments

AMONG → trong một nhóm
→ among the employees
→ among three candidate

6. TO / FROM
FROM → điểm bắt đầu
TO   → điểm đến / người nhận

→ from Hanoi to Da Nang
→ from Monday to Friday
→ send the form to HR
