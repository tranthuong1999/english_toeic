7.6 — Conjunction vs Adverbial Connector
* but vs however
Cả hai đều có ý “nhưng / tuy nhiên”, nhưng cấu trúc khác nhau.
S + V , but S+ V
S+V . However, S + V.

EX: The product is expensive, but many customers buy it. ✅
The product is expensive. However, many customers buy it. ✅
* so vs therefore
S + V, so S + V.
S + V. Therefore, S + V.
The weather was bad, so the flight was delayed. ✅
The weather was bad. Therefore, the flight was delayed. ✅

7. Mẹo làm Part 5 ⭐⭐⭐

Nhìn ngay sau chỗ trống:

S + V → nghĩ Conjunction
N / V-ing → nghĩ Preposition / cấu trúc tương ứng
Sau đó xác định quan hệ:
nguyên nhân → because
tương phản → although
thời gian → when/while/before/after
điều kiện → if/unless
mục đích → so that
🎯 Công thức vàng

because + S V ↔ because of + N

although + S V ↔ despite + N/V-ing

while + S V ↔ during + N

if + S V ↔ unless + S V
