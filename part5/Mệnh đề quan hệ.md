WHERE / WHEN / WHY — Phân biệt với which + giới từ
This is the office where I work.
Có thể viết:
This is the office in which I work.

2. WHEN = in/on which
Dùng khi danh từ trước là thời gian.
2020 was the year when the company was founded.
=
2020 was the year in which the company was founded.
3. WHY = for which
Nobody knows the reason why he left. = Nobody knows the reason for which he left.
| Ý nghĩa   | Cách thường gặp | Cách tương đương |
| --------- | --------------- | ---------------- |
| Nơi chốn  | **where**       | in/at which      |
| Thời gian | **when**        | in/on which      |
| Lý do     | **why**         | for which        |

người → whom

who + V → S
S + V + whom → O

The company ___ employees work remotely is expanding.

employees = N

Bẫy 3 — where

The office ___ I work is downtown.

office = nơi chốn

→ where ✅

Bẫy 4 — dấu phẩy + that

Mr. Brown, ___ works here, is my manager.

Có dấu phẩy → Non-defining

→ that ❌

→ người → who ✅
